Two editions

One ecosystem. Two engine implementations.

Luma Engine is developed in two distinct directions. Each edition shares the same Sakura Dark identity, but has its own architecture, tooling, and maturity. Neither is a replacement for the other — both are real, independent implementations.

◇ C++ EDITION

Luma Engine C++

Classic · Pale lavender

The original Luma Engine: a hand-crafted C++20 codebase with an integrated editor, software rasterization pipeline, LOVE scripting VM, scene graph, and audio system.

Technology

The pieces behind the engine.

Luma Engine is not a single product. It is a collection of technology projects that share a philosophy: software that respects the hardware it runs on.

Dimension L

The C++ rendering technology at the core of Luma Engine. Software rasterization, a modular render graph, and the DL-V1 architecture.

DL-ARA

The modular Rust workspace. A collection of crates covering math, input, rasterization, ray tracing, render graphs, and the launcher.

LumaX

A dedicated physics engine project within the Luma ecosystem. In active development. Scope and maturity are documented on its own page.
