Octo Spacers

Arcade shooter · Particle-heavy chaos

A fast-paced arcade shooter that exists to stress-test the engine. Every enemy, every bullet, every explosion is drawn by the software rasterizer — no GPU, no cheating. Explores particle systems, sprite batching, and the limits of 60 FPS on modest hardware.
